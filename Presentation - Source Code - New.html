Smart Photo Delivery

Scan your face.
Get your photos.

Guests scan a QR code at the event, open the face scan page, and instantly receive every photo the photographer captured of them — no searching, no album digging.

01

Guest scans the event QR code

A unique QR code is placed at the venue — on tables, walls, or the welcome board. One scan opens the photo portal.

02

Face scan on their phone

The browser opens the camera. The guest looks into it for 3 seconds. No app download required — fully web-based.

03

AI matches face to photo library

The system compares the scan against all photos uploaded by the photographer and returns every matched image instantly.

04

Guest downloads their photos

A personal gallery of all matched photos is shown. One tap to download all — or share directly to WhatsApp and Instagram.

05

Photographer integration — plug & play

Photographers with their own face recognition system connect via our open API. We provide a simple webhook — their system pushes photos, ours handles delivery.

🔒 Privacy first. Face data is processed in real time and never stored. Only matched photo files are returned. Guests opt in before scanning. Fully GDPR-aligned.
